Gabbage collected once a week, paid once a month

Bin Go Sacco has collected gabbage on the Ruiru road since 2024. Around three hundred households on ruiru road use our collection service once a week and we collect for every household that has paid.

Any household on one of our six routes may join. You need a national ID and a bank account, and you may start putting out your garbage from the day your membership number is issued.

Where we collect

  • Kamiti route
  • Juja route
  • Ruiru route
  • Membly route
  • Githurai route
  • Kiambu route

Notices

  • Gabbage is collected once a week KSh 100.00 per month from 3 November 2026.
  • Annual General Meeting: Saturday 17 November, 10 a.m., at the Waste Transfer centre.
  • New members: apply to join. You need a national ID and a bank account.
