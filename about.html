fifteen workers and ten garbage trucks

The company was registered in 2024 by 15 workers who were collecting trash at the roadside and enhancing sanitation in Ruiru They bought three garbage trucks between them and put them alongside different routes at Ruiru, Kiambu County

We now collect on three routes and pay around twenty five hundred members every month. The garbage trucks at Ruiru are still there. Everything else about how the sacco runs has been rebuilt at least once, and the ledger that used to be a school exercise book is the reason this site exists.

We started with fifteen workers and ten garbage trucks. The ledger was a school exercise book.
